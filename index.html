🚀 Mission: The Invisible Interface

Year 5 Coding Lab | Master the Camera & Sound

Level 1

📹 Video Motion

Teach your computer to feel movement. Use the pixels in your camera as a giant touch-screen!

Activity: Ghost Hunter
Make a Ghost hide only if you wave your hand really fast (Motion > 80).

🌎 Real World: Automatic hand dryers in the bathroom use sensors just like this!

Level 2

🎭 Face Sensing

Use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to find your nose, eyes, and mouth. It’s like magic tracking!

Activity: Digital Mask
Make a "Crown" sprite stay on top of your head as you dance around the room.

🌎 Real World: This is how Snapchat filters put dog ears on your head!

Level 3

🎵 Music & Rhythm

Don't just play a sound—be the composer! Use code to change instruments and play notes.

Activity: Be the DJ
Every time you pop a bubble, play a random note (1 to 100) on a "Electric Guitar."

🌎 Real World: DJs use "Launchpads" which are computers that turn touches into music.

🏆 FINAL BOSS CHALLENGE: "The Rockstar Drummer"

Can you combine all three skills into one mega-project?

  • The Drums: 3 sprites at the top that play drum sounds when your hand "hits" them (Video Motion).
  • The Style: A Rockstar Wig that follows your head (Face Sensing).
  • The Crowd: Every time you hit a drum, the "Audience" sprite cheers and changes color (Events).
